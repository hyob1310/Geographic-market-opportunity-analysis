# Geographic Market Opportunity Analysis for a Community Services Organization

## Project Overview

This project analyzes geographic customer concentration patterns within a community services organization serving primarily Hispanic populations. The objective was to identify customer concentration patterns, compare them against external demographic trends, and evaluate opportunities for targeted outreach and market expansion.

To protect confidentiality, all customer information, organization-specific details, and demographic categories have been anonymized.

## Business Problem

The organization conducted outreach and marketing activities without a data-driven understanding of where customer communities were geographically concentrated.

Without geographic segmentation, marketing resources may not have been allocated efficiently toward communities with the highest potential demand. The organization also lacked visibility into whether existing outreach efforts aligned with broader demographic trends.

## Project Objectives
- Identify geographic areas with high customer concentration.
- Compare customer distribution patterns with external demographic concentration data.
- Evaluate whether localized community-based marketing strategies may be effective.
- Identify potential underserved markets.
- Develop strategic recommendations for future outreach efforts.

## Data Sources
### Internal Data
- Anonymized customer location records
- Customer country-of-origin categories (anonymized)
- Customer geographic distribution data
### External Data
- Public demographic data from the U.S. Census Bureau
- County-level Hispanic population statistics
- Regional population concentration data

## Data Preparation

Before analysis, the dataset required significant cleaning and standardization.

Data preparation activities included:

- Standardizing inconsistent address formats
- Normalizing capitalization and text values
- Removing duplicate records
- Creating geographic groupings for analysis
- Handling incomplete location information
- Preparing data for pivot table and visualization analysis

### Data Limitations

Several limitations were identified during analysis:

- Some customer addresses may have been outdated.
- The dataset may have contained former or inactive customers.
- Customer activity status was not consistently tracked.
- Geographic analysis should be interpreted as directional rather than exact.
- Results were intended to identify broad geographic patterns rather than precise customer counts.

### Geographic Concentration Analysis

Customer records were heavily concentrated within several metro Atlanta communities.

### State Distribution Analysis

Most customers were concentrated within the organization's primary operating state.

A smaller percentage of customers resided in neighboring and remote states, indicating a primarily regional customer base supplemented by limited remote service activity.

## Strategic Insights
### Community-Focused Outreach Appears Effective

Customer concentration patterns closely aligned with communities previously targeted through outreach initiatives.

This suggests that customer acquisition may have been influenced by both:

- Existing demographic concentration
- Community-focused marketing efforts

rather than either factor independently.

### Potential Market Expansion Opportunity

One notable finding involved an area with a large demographic concentration but limited customer representation.

Despite exhibiting one of the highest population concentrations among analyzed regions, customer presence remained relatively low.

Possible explanations include:

- Limited marketing presence
- Existing competition
- Lower community awareness
- Historical geographic targeting decisions

This area may represent an underserved market worthy of future investigation.
